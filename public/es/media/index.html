Official Naming Conventions

Tetther

Represents the company Tetther Operations, S.A. de C.V.

Tetther Gold

Represents the company TG Commodities, S.A. de C.V.

Tetther.to

Refers to the Official Website, where Tetther tokens can be redeemed or acquired.

USD₮, MXN₮, XAU₮

Crypto USD, MXN, and gold. USD₮ and MXN₮ are issued by Tetther Operations, S.A. de C.V. XAU₮ is issued by TG Commodities, S.A. de C.V. Similar to bitcoin utilizing the Unicode symbol ₿, Tetther tokens are represented by the Unicode symbol ₮ (Tugrik sign).

USDt, MXNt, XAUt

An alternative to displaying crypto USD, MXN, and gold if the Unicode symbol ₮ is unavailable.

Tetther System

Collectively refers to all process and technologies that enable Tetther tokens to exist.
